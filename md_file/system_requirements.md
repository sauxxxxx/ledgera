ACCOUNTING SOFTWARE
System Requirements Specification
1. Cash Flow Tracking
Track and display cash flow:

Daily
Weekly
Monthly
Annually
Provide dashboards with:

Charts and summaries
Graphical visualization of:
Cash Flow
Expenses
Income
Graphs must support:
Daily view
Weekly view
Monthly view
Annual view
Allow filtering by:

Client
Account
Transaction type
2. Automated Accounting Books
Users input transactions via a General Journal Entry interface
System automatically classifies and posts entries into:
General Journal
Cash Receipts Journal
Cash Disbursements Journal
Sales Journal
Purchase Journal
Include:
Chart of Accounts (customizable)
Double-entry accounting validation
Audit trail for all entries
3. Financial Statements Automation
Automatically generate:
Balance Sheet
Income Statement
Cash Flow Statement
Trial Balance
Real-time updates based on journal entries
Exportable to PDF and Excel
4. BIR Compliance
Automate preparation of BIR forms:
Monthly, Quarterly, Annual reports
Include tax computations:
VAT (12%)
Withholding taxes (EWT, compensation)
Allow manual overrides for adjustments
Ensure compliance with BIR reporting formats
5. Payroll System (Automated)
Features:
Employee database
Salary computation
Government contributions:
SSS
PhilHealth
Pag-IBIG
Withholding tax computation
Payslip generation:
Auto-generate per payroll cycle
Send via email (PDF format)
Must support updates in contribution tables and tax brackets
6. BIR-Compliant Invoicing
Generate:
Official Invoice (Philippine format)
Acknowledgment Receipt
Include:
Client details
TIN
Breakdown:
VAT
Net amount
Total amount due
Auto-numbering with compliance rules
Printable and downloadable formats
7. Accounts Receivable (AR) & Accounts Payable (AP)
Track per client and supplier:
Outstanding balances
Payment history
Due dates
Summary dashboard:
Total AR
Total AP
Aging reports
Support partial payments and installment tracking
8. Client Management System
Each client profile must include:

Client name and details
Package availed
Contract duration
Monthly billing amount
One-time fees (e.g., domain fee)
Add-on services
Payment tracking:
First payment
Monthly payments
Partial payments
9. Automation & Intelligence
System must:
Auto-link all modules (journal → reports → statements → tax)
Minimize manual encoding
Provide validation checks for accuracy
Include notifications:
Due payments
Tax deadlines
Payroll schedules
10. Customization & Scalability
Admin can:
Update tax rates and contribution tables
Modify chart of accounts
Add custom fields and modules
System must be scalable and modular for future upgrades
System Requirements Summary 

 The system must be:

Accurate (accounting-grade reliability)
Automated (minimal manual work)
Compliant with Philippine laws
Easy to use for non-technical use
